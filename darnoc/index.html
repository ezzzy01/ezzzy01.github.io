Final-year project · E-commerce

A computer shop, open online.

Darnoc IT is a web store for a computer hardware shop in Melaka. Customers shop, build a PC that fits together, trade in old parts and get warranty support. Staff and admins run orders, stock and promotions from their own dashboards.

Why it was built

The shop sold through chats. That doesn't scale.

01

Orders lived in chat

Every order, payment and delivery was agreed by hand in social media messages.

A cart, checkout and an order history that both the customer and the shop can follow.

02

"Will these parts work together?"

Customers had to ask before buying, and a wrong CPU or motherboard meant a return.

A PC builder that checks the CPU socket against the motherboard and keeps a running total.

03

Trade-ins and promotions by hand

Offers for used parts and discounts were tracked in messages and memory.

Trade-in requests with photos and a staff decision, and promotions that show everywhere at once.

What it does

One store, three kinds of user.

Each role signs in to its own dashboard. In the demo you can switch between them from the bar at the top.

Build a PC that fits together

Pick a processor, motherboard, memory, graphics card, storage, power supply and case. The builder flags a CPU that doesn't fit the motherboard's socket, shows stock and keeps the total, then puts the whole build in the cart.

  • Socket check between CPU and motherboard
  • Live price with promotions applied
  • One click to add the build to the cart

How it's built

Plain PHP pages and a MySQL database.

Data and privacy

Made-up data, and nothing leaves your tab.

Made-up data

Every customer, order and ticket in the demo is invented. No real shop data is used.

Stays in your tab

The demo keeps changes in this browser tab only. Close it, or press Reset demo, and it starts fresh.

Passwords hashed

The real app stores passwords as hashes, never as text. For a live shop I'd move to PHP's password_hash with a salt.

Safe queries

Prepared statements (PDO and mysqli) keep user input out of the SQL.

Role checks

Each page checks the signed-in role, so a customer can't open staff or admin pages.

Questions

Good to know.

Is the demo the real code?

Not quite. GitHub Pages can't run PHP or MySQL, so the demo rebuilds the same pages and flows in HTML and JavaScript with made-up data. The layout, colours and steps match the real app.

What can I try?

Shop, build a PC, check out, trade in a part and open a ticket as a customer; then switch to Staff or Admin to process the order, answer the ticket and make an offer.

Will I break anything?

No. Nothing is sent anywhere. Press Reset demo in the top bar, or close the tab, to start again.

Is Darnoc IT a real shop?

Yes, a computer hardware shop in Melaka. This was built for it as my final-year project; the demo's products, prices and people are made up.

Can I see the source code?

The repository is private. Email me and I can walk you through it.

Does it work on a phone?

Yes. Both the real app and the demo fit a phone screen.

Click around the store yourself.

Shop as a customer, then run the same order as staff or admin. It takes about two minutes.
